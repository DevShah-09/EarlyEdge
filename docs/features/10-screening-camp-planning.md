# Feature: Screening Camp Planning

## Purpose

Aggregates patient risk by ward, estimates required outreach camps, recommends screening types, and schedules camp records.

## Planning formulas

For each ward:

```text
high_risk_count = count(risk_tier == "High")
medium_risk_count = count(risk_tier == "Medium")
recommended_camps = ceil(high_risk_count / CAMP_CAPACITY)
NNS = total_patients / high_risk_count       (0 when no High-risk patients)
coverage_percent = high_risk_count / total_patients * 100
```

`CAMP_CAPACITY` defaults to 15. Plans are sorted by absolute High-risk count, not by NNS. Although lower NNS generally indicates greater yield, the current field describes the ratio within already-known uploaded patients, not the epidemiological number needed to screen in an unscreened population.

## Suggested screenings

Every ward receives Blood Pressure Check and BMI Measurement. Based on mean model scores:

- mean diabetes risk > 40: Fasting Blood Glucose and “HbA1c AI Screening”;
- mean CVD risk > 40: Lipid Profile and ECG Referral;
- mean hypertension risk > 40: Cardiovascular Stress Test.

Duplicates are removed while preserving insertion order. These suggestions are hard-coded, not generated by AI or a guideline engine.

## UI and endpoints

`ScreeningCampsPage.jsx` loads `GET /api/camps/plan` and `GET /api/camps` concurrently. It displays ward plans, NNS summaries, recommended screenings, upcoming/past camps, month selection, and camp creation.

`POST /api/camps` stores ward, start/end dates, venue, target, Planned status, and selected screenings. Staffing, cost, and outcomes exist in the schema but are not calculated by the planner or collected in the create request.

## Date and filter behavior

The `month` query is only echoed as a response label; it does not filter patient visits or camps. The frontend service supports `ward`, but the backend planner does not accept it. Upcoming versus past uses whether `end_date` is greater than or equal to a current ISO timestamp even though the field is a SQL DATE.

## Failure behavior

Planning catches all exceptions and returns an empty response with `month="Error"` and HTTP 200. This can look like a genuine zero-risk state unless the UI checks the label.

## Limitations

No geographic distance, population denominator, venue availability, staff availability, budget optimization, overlap/deduplication, capacity by screening type, date validation (`end >= start`), or outcome-update endpoint is implemented.
